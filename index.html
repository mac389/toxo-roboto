Practice the call before it counts.

Toxo Roboto helps fellows in medical toxicology learn how to field Poison Center calls. Synthetic cases, real results.

How it works:

  1. Get an access code. You can try a case for free by answering a tox question
  2. Toxo Roboto will call you
  3. You and the attendings do tape review

Designed with boundaries

A focused educational environment

01

Synthetic cases

Scenarios are created for training. Users should never enter real patient identifiers or protected health information.

02

Clear disclosure

Phone-based simulations identify themselves as AI-generated training calls and require confirmation before beginning.

03

Education, not care

Toxo Roboto is not a poison center, medical device, clinical decision engine, or substitute for emergency services.

Not for patient care

Toxo Roboto provides simulated educational experiences only. For an actual poisoning or exposure in the United States, contact Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222. For an emergency, call 911.
