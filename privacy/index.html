Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026

Overview

Toxo Roboto is an educational application for simulated poison-center calls. This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit this public website or use the private training application.

Do not submit real patient information, protected health information, or details from an actual clinical case.

Information we process

The public informational website does not include analytics, advertising trackers, account registration, or forms. GitHub may process technical information needed to deliver GitHub Pages under its own privacy statement.

The training application may process:

  • Account information supplied by the hosting platform, such as a user identifier, name, email address, and role.
  • Simulation selections, typed or transcribed conversation turns, scoring results, debriefs, call duration, and timestamps.
  • Microphone audio while a voice session is active. The pilot is not designed to retain raw audio recordings.
  • For opted-in telephone simulations, the destination telephone number and operational call metadata needed to place and manage the call.
  • Basic security, diagnostic, and request information generated by hosting and communications providers.

How information is used

Information is used to provide the simulation, generate caller responses and educational feedback, save authorized tape reviews, operate telephone calls, secure the service, diagnose failures, and improve the prototype.

Toxo Roboto does not sell personal information or use simulation content for targeted advertising.

Service providers

The service may rely on GitHub for this public website, OpenAI for AI and realtime audio processing, Twilio for opted-in telephone calls, and the application hosting provider for identity, storage, and delivery. These providers process information under their respective terms and privacy policies.

Retention and choices

Saved simulation transcripts and debriefs may remain available until they are deleted or the pilot’s retention process removes them. Provider operational records may follow separate retention schedules. Users may stop microphone processing by ending or pausing the voice session and may decline a telephone simulation before it begins.

Security and appropriate use

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used for this prototype, but no internet service is completely secure. Toxo Roboto is intended for adult clinicians and trainees and is not directed to children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the pilot changes. The effective date above will be revised when material updates are published.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the Toxo Roboto project through GitHub. GitHub issues are public: do not include patient information, health information, telephone numbers, credentials, or other sensitive personal data.
